Privacy Policy — Qiraat Pipeline

Last updated: 28 October 2026

Qiraat Pipeline is a private, single-user tool operated by the owner of the YouTube channel and Facebook Page "قراءة المشهد" (Qiraat Al-Mashhad). It is not offered to the public.

What the tool does

It uploads videos produced by the channel owner to the owner's own YouTube channel (YouTube Data API v3) and Facebook Page (Meta Graph API), sets their titles, descriptions, tags, thumbnails and publish times, and reads basic information about the owner's own channel (channel name and upload status).

Data accessed and stored

  • OAuth access/refresh tokens for the owner's own Google and Facebook accounts, stored only on the owner's computer and never shared.
  • No personal data of viewers or any other user is collected, stored, sold or shared.

Use of Google user data

Qiraat Pipeline's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. By using it, the owner agrees to the YouTube Terms of Service; see also the Google Privacy Policy.

Revoking access

Access can be revoked at any time at Google security settings and in Facebook Business Integrations settings.
